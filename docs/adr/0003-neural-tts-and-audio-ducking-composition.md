# 0003. Neural TTS and Audio Ducking Composition

## Context
Commercial video demands broadcast-standard audio with clear spoken dialogue, immersive background music, and punchy sound effects. If music volume remains static, voiceover clarity is obscured; conversely, abrupt cuts in background music destroy pacing.

## Decision
Implement a multi-track audio architecture using Neural TTS (Vietnamese voiceover tailored to KOL persona) with per-beat audio stems, paired with an FFmpeg complex filter graph performing automated audio ducking. The background music is attenuated by a nominal -14dB (see Amendment) during active voiceover segments and ramps back smoothly during pauses. Direct-to-camera presenter shots enforce lip-sync alignment and timing constraints (<50ms offset).

## Consequences
- Voiceover dialogue is punchy and intelligible (-14 LUFS standard) while maintaining energetic music rhythm.
- Audio stems are isolated for modular replacement without requiring video re-render.

## Amendment: ducking depth and shot timing
- **Ducking depth is -14 dB nominal, not -24 dB.** The original -24 dB figure was never what the code did (the constant said -14 dB and the old filter, `ratio=4, mix=0.75, attack=200ms`, dipped the music only 0.3 to 7.6 dB). A sidechain compressor's depth depends on how loud the voice key is, so no single figure is exact. Measured with real ffmpeg (pink-noise bed at the composer's -12 dB gain, band-limited noise as speech), `DUCKING_FILTER` (`ratio=20`, 30 ms attack, 500 ms release) dips the bed 11.4 dB for quiet speech (-32 dB mean), 21 dB for typical TTS (-22 dB mean) and 29.5 dB for loud speech. -14 dB is the design floor the tests enforce (>= 10 dB); a -24 dB fixed duck would bury the music bed under every beat and was rejected. `BGM_DUCKING_ATTENUATION_DB = -14.0` in `pipeline/composer.py` is the single source of truth and is what `edit_decisions.json` and `audio_mix_report.json` record.
- **Shots are cut to the beat, never padded with frozen frames.** Video models return fixed 6 s or 10 s clips while beats last 1.5 to 6 s, and the pre-muxed synced clip froze its last frame whenever the voice outlasted the footage (measured: 5.1 s of a 32.5 s master frozen). When every beat has its raw clip and voice stem, the composer re-cuts each shot: `duration = max(beat estimated_duration, voice stem length)`, taken from real motion at the start of the clip; the voice stem is padded with silence or trimmed to the shot. Only when the clip is genuinely shorter is it slowed with `setpts`, up to `MAX_SLOWDOWN = 1.25x` (the soft beat estimate is capped there; the voice is never cut off); a frozen tail is the last resort when even 1.25x cannot cover the voice. Each decision (`action` = `exact|trim|slowdown|slowdown_and_hold`, `in_point`, `out_point`, `slowdown_factor`, `held_tail_seconds`, timeline start/end) is written per shot to `edit_decisions.json` under `shots`. Without raw clips and stems the composer falls back to the synced clips unchanged (`action: passthrough`).
